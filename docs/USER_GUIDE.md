# RunDay User Guide

## 🏃‍♂️ Welcome to RunDay!

RunDay is your ultimate platform for discovering, registering for, and tracking running events. Whether you're a casual jogger or a competitive runner, RunDay helps you stay connected with the running community.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. **Visit the RunDay Platform**: Go to [https://run-day-user.vercel.app](https://run-day-user.vercel.app)
2. **Sign Up**: Click "Sign Up" and enter your email and password
3. **Verify Email**: Check your email and click the verification link
4. **Complete Profile**: Add your personal information and running preferences

### Navigating the Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Upcoming Events**: Events you've registered for
- **Recent Activity**: Your latest registrations and results  
- **Quick Stats**: Total events, completion rate, and achievements
- **Recommended Events**: Personalized event suggestions

## 📅 Finding and Registering for Events

### Browse Events

1. **Visit Events Page**: Click "Events" in the navigation
2. **Use Filters**: Filter by:
   - Distance (5K, 10K, Half Marathon, Marathon)
   - Date range
   - Location
   - Registration status
3. **Search**: Use the search bar to find specific events

### Event Registration

1. **View Event Details**: Click on any event card
2. **Check Requirements**: Review distance, date, and location
3. **Register**: Click "Register Now" button
4. **Confirmation**: You'll receive immediate confirmation

### Managing Registrations

- **View Registrations**: Go to "My Events" → "Upcoming"
- **Cancel Registration**: Click "Cancel Registration" (if allowed)
- **Event Reminders**: Check your dashboard for upcoming events

## 🏆 Results and Certificates

### Viewing Results

After completing an event:
1. **Go to My Events**: Click "My Events" → "Completed"
2. **View Results**: See your finish time, position, and performance
3. **Track Progress**: Compare with previous events

### Downloading Certificates

1. **Complete an Event**: Participate and finish the event
2. **Wait for Results**: Admin will input your results
3. **Generate Certificate**: 
   - Go to "Certificates" in navigation
   - Click "Generate Certificate" for completed events
   - Preview and download your PDF certificate

### Certificate Features

Your certificates include:
- **Personal Details**: Name, event info, finish time
- **Performance Badge**: Winner, Podium, or Finisher designation
- **Professional Design**: High-quality PDF format
- **Permanent Storage**: Access anytime from your account

## 📊 Tracking Your Progress

### Event History

- **Performance Metrics**: Track improvement over time
- **Event Statistics**: Total events, average times, best performances
- **Achievement Badges**: Earn badges for milestones
- **Export Data**: Download your complete running history

### Personal Dashboard

Monitor your running journey with:
- **Total Events**: Complete count of registered/completed events
- **Recent Activity**: Latest registrations and achievements
- **Upcoming Events**: Next events on your calendar
- **Performance Trends**: Visual progress tracking

## ⚙️ Account Management

### Profile Settings

Update your information:
- **Personal Details**: Name, email, contact information
- **Running Preferences**: Favorite distances, goals
- **Privacy Settings**: Control visibility of your activities

### Notifications

Manage email notifications for:
- **Event Reminders**: Upcoming event alerts
- **Registration Confirmations**: Immediate confirmation emails
- **Results Notifications**: When results are available
- **New Events**: Notifications about new events in your area

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't Sign In?**
- Check email/password spelling
- Verify your email address was confirmed
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed

**Registration Problems?**
- Ensure event registration is open
- Check if event is full
- Verify you meet event requirements

**Missing Certificates?**
- Ensure you completed the event
- Check if results have been posted
- Contact event organizer if needed

### Getting Help

- **Contact Support**: Use the contact form on the website
- **Event Questions**: Contact the event organizer directly
- **Technical Issues**: Report bugs through the feedback system
